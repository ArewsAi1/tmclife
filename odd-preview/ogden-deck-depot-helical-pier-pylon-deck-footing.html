Deck foundation systems

Helical Pier and Pylon Deck Footings

Helical deck footings can provide a practical foundation option where access, soil conditions, excavation or installation speed make traditional concrete footings difficult.

Discuss your projectRequest information

Why consider helical footings?

Reduced excavation

Many systems install with less digging and site disturbance than large poured concrete footings.

Immediate load capacity

Depending on the engineered system, framing may proceed without waiting for concrete curing.

Difficult-site flexibility

Useful for slopes, limited access and locations where conventional footing work is challenging.

Planning considerations

  • Local code and permit requirements
  • Soil and frost-depth conditions
  • Deck loads and footing spacing
  • Manufacturer installation requirements
  • Engineering documentation when required

Match the footing to the plan

Footings are structural components. Final sizing, quantity and placement should follow approved plans, manufacturer specifications and local building requirements.
