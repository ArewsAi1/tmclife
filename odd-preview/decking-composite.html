Why Choose Composite Decking?

Composite decking offers numerous benefits over traditional wood decking, making it a popular choice for homeowners and contractors alike. Here are some reasons to consider composite decking for your next project:
  • Low Maintenance: Composite decking requires minimal upkeep, saving you time and effort.
  • Durability: Resistant to weather, insects, and decay, composite decking provides long-lasting performance.
  • Eco-Friendly: Made from recycled materials, composite decking is a sustainable choice for environmentally conscious homeowners.
  • Variety: Available in a wide range of colors, textures, and finishes, composite decking allows for endless design possibilities.

​

Frequently asked question about composite decking:

What is composite decking made of?

Composite decking is made from a blend of wood fibers, plastic, and bonding agents. The mixture is heated, formed into board shapes, and cooled to create a durable and low-maintenance decking material.

How does composite decking compare to wood decking?

Composite decking is more resistant to weather, rot, and insects than traditional wood decking. It requires less maintenance, doesn’t need staining or sealing, and typically has a longer lifespan.

Is composite decking eco-friendly?

Yes, composite decking is considered eco-friendly because it often contains recycled materials like reclaimed wood fibers and recycled plastic. This reduces the demand for virgin materials and helps divert waste from landfills.

How long does composite decking last?

Composite decking can last 25-30 years or more, depending on the brand, quality, and how well it is maintained. It typically comes with extensive warranties, often ranging from 25 to 50 years.

Does composite decking require maintenance?

Composite decking requires minimal maintenance compared to wood. It typically only needs regular cleaning with soap and water to remove dirt and debris. There’s no need for sanding, staining, or sealing.

Is composite decking slip-resistant?

Many composite decking products are designed with textured surfaces to enhance slip resistance. However, it’s important to check specific product features and ratings for slip resistance if this is a concern.

​Can composite decking be painted or stained?

Composite decking is designed to maintain its color and finish without the need for painting or staining. Attempting to paint or stain it may void the warranty and could affect its performance.

How much does composite decking cost compared to wood?

Composite decking generally has a higher upfront cost compared to traditional wood decking. However, its lower maintenance requirements and longer lifespan can make it more cost-effective over time.

Is composite decking easy to install?

Composite decking can be installed using standard woodworking tools. Many brands offer hidden fastener systems and detailed installation guides, making the process straightforward. However, hiring a professional installer can ensure optimal results.
