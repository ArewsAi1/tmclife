Deck Rot Repair in Ogden

Rot can affect visible decking or hidden structural components where moisture remains trapped. Ogden Decks checks the surrounding framing and connection points before replacing damaged material.

Repairing the source

Effective repair may include removing deteriorated wood, improving drainage or ventilation, correcting flashing and replacing affected boards, joists, posts, trim or stair components.
