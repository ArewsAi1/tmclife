What to Consider When Hiring a Deck Builder

A useful proposal should explain what is being built, which materials are included, who handles permits and inspections, how changes are approved and what documentation is provided after completion.

Verify before signing

Confirm current licensing and insurance directly, review recent relevant projects, compare structural details rather than totals alone, and make sure payment stages and exclusions are written clearly.

Ask about the complete system

Decking is only one part of the project. Foundations, framing, ledger connections, stairs, railing, drainage, fascia and access all affect performance.
