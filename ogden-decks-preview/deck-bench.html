Built-In Deck Benches in Ogden

Built-in benches add permanent seating without requiring separate furniture in every part of the deck.

Integrated design

Bench height, depth, back support, traffic flow and railing requirements are planned together. Materials can match or complement the main deck surface.

New decks and upgrades

Benches can be included in a new build or added when the existing framing can support the new loads and attachment points.
