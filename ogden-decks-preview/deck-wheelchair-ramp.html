Deck Wheelchair Ramp Planning in Ogden

A usable ramp requires more than adding a sloped walkway. Rise, available run, landings, turning space, railing and the connection to doors and walkways all affect the design.

Site-specific layouts

Ogden Decks evaluates the deck height, yard space, drainage, snow exposure and access route before recommending a ramp configuration.

Code and accessibility goals

Residential accessibility needs vary by project. Final dimensions and guard requirements are coordinated with applicable local rules and the homeowner's access goals.
