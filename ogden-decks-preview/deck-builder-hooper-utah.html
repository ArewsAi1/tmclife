Planning Decks for Hooper Properties

Hooper homes often have larger yards, open exposure and room for multi-zone outdoor spaces. Good planning considers sun, wind, drainage, access from the house and how the deck will connect to lawns, patios, pools or future landscaping.

Projects can include ground-level decks, elevated decks, expansions, replacement decking, hot-tub platforms and entertainment areas. Framing, footings, stair placement and railing are planned together so the finished space functions as one system.
