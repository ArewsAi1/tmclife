Deck Footings and Helical Piers

A dependable deck begins with a foundation suited to the structure, soil and site conditions.

Foundation options

Depending on engineering and local requirements, projects may use concrete footings or helical pier systems. Helical piers can be useful on slopes, in difficult soils or where excavation access is limited.

Planned with the structure

Footing placement, beam spans, post loads, frost depth and drainage must be coordinated as one structural system.
