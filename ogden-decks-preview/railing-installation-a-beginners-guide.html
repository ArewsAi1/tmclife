Railing Installation: A Homeowner Guide

Railing is a complete safety system made up of posts, rails, infill, fasteners and connections to the deck structure.

Start with layout and structure

Post spacing, stair transitions, gate locations and attachment points should be planned before decking or trim blocks access.

Choose compatible components

Steel, aluminum, cable, vinyl and composite systems have different post, infill and fastening requirements.
