Decks in Winter in Ogden

Northern Utah decks must handle snow accumulation, freeze-thaw cycles, shaded ice and repeated moisture. Good planning starts with drainage, sound framing and safe access.

Winter deck considerations

Use material-appropriate snow removal, avoid products that can damage the surface, keep exits clear and inspect stairs, railing and drainage areas as conditions change.
