Deck Permits in Ogden City

Permit requirements depend on deck height, attachment, stairs, guards, setbacks and project scope. Ogden Decks helps organize plans and construction details so the project can move through the local review and inspection process.

What may be reviewed

Site placement, footing depth, beam and joist sizing, ledger attachment, stair geometry, railing height and required inspections may all be part of the permit process.
