Deck Repair Services in Ogden

Deck repairs should begin with the cause of the problem, not only the visible damage. Ogden Decks evaluates boards, stairs, railing, framing, ledger connections, posts, beams and moisture exposure.

Repair or replacement

Targeted repairs may be appropriate when the primary structure remains serviceable. More extensive deterioration, movement or connection failure may require partial or complete replacement.
