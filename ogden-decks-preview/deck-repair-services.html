Start With the Cause of the Damage

A reliable deck repair should address more than the most visible board or loose fastener. Ogden Decks evaluates the walking surface, stairs, railing, ledger connection, joists, beams, posts, footings and areas where water may be trapped against the home or framing.

Common warning signs include soft or splintering boards, sagging sections, movement underfoot, rusted fasteners, cracked posts, loose handrails, separated stair stringers, discoloration around the ledger and recurring moisture damage.

After the evaluation, we explain which components can be repaired, which should be replaced and whether the remaining structure is suitable for resurfacing or upgrades.

Repair, Resurface or Replace?

Targeted repairs are often appropriate when the primary structure is stable and damage is limited to specific boards, stairs, railing or isolated framing. Resurfacing may make sense when the joists, beams, posts and footings remain dependable but the visible surface is worn or outdated.

Partial or complete replacement may be safer when deterioration is widespread, the deck moves excessively, ledger or footing conditions are poor, or the existing structure cannot support the desired new materials and features. We provide a practical explanation of the options instead of assuming every deck requires full replacement.

Composite Deck Repairs

Trex, Deckorators and other composite decks may need board replacement, fascia correction, fastener repairs, stair work or railing upgrades. Color matching depends on the product line, age and exposure. When an exact match is unavailable, picture framing, accent boards or a larger resurfacing plan can create a deliberate finished appearance.
