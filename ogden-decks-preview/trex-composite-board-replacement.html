Trex Composite Board Replacement in Ogden

Individual composite boards may need replacement after impact damage, heat damage, staining or fastening problems. Ogden Decks checks the existing board profile, framing and fastener system before removing decking.

Matching and replacement

We identify the closest available Trex line and color, then explain whether isolated replacement, a blended repair or a larger resurfacing section will produce the best result.

Underlying conditions

Board damage can sometimes reveal spacing, moisture or framing issues that should be corrected before new decking is installed.
