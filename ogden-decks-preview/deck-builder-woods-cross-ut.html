Material and Maintenance Choices

Composite decking reduces routine staining and sealing, while wood offers a traditional appearance and can be refinished. Railing, fascia and border details should be coordinated with the decking and the exterior of the home.

For older decks, visible board wear may not show the full condition of the structure. Posts, beams, joists, ledger connections, stairs and moisture exposure should be reviewed before deciding between repair and replacement.
