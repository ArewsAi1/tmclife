Deck Stair Repair in Ogden

Deck stairs can become unsafe when treads loosen, stringers split, landings move or handrails lose secure attachment. Ogden Decks evaluates the complete stair assembly before recommending repair or replacement.

Common stair problems

Repairs may address damaged treads, inconsistent rise and run, weak stringers, loose railing, rot, poor landing support and movement where the stairs connect to the deck.
