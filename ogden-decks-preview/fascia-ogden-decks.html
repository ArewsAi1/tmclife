Deck Fascia Installation in Ogden

Deck fascia finishes the exposed perimeter of a deck and helps create a clean transition between the walking surface, framing and stairs.

Fascia options

Ogden Decks installs composite and wood fascia, replaces damaged edge boards and coordinates fascia details with picture framing, stair stringers and railing posts.

Repair and replacement

Loose, warped or deteriorated fascia can be replaced after the framing behind it is checked for moisture damage or movement.
