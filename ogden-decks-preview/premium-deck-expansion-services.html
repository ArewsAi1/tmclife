Deck Expansion Services in Ogden

A deck expansion can create more room for dining, seating, stairs, shade structures or a hot-tub area without rebuilding every usable part of the existing deck.

Expansion planning

Ogden Decks evaluates the current framing, footings, ledger, railing and traffic flow before designing the new section. Materials and trim details are coordinated so the addition feels intentional.

When an expansion makes sense

Expansion is considered when the existing structure remains serviceable and the new loads, spans and connections can be supported safely.
