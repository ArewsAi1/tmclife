Repair Versus Replacement

Localized damage may be repaired when the primary structure remains sound. Widespread rot, movement, connection failure or outdated framing can make replacement the better long-term choice. A proper evaluation should include posts, beams, joists, ledger connections, stairs and railing—not only the visible deck boards.

Materials and Maintenance

Composite decking offers lower routine maintenance and a wide range of color options. Wood decking provides a traditional appearance and can be refinished, but it requires periodic cleaning, staining and sealing. We help homeowners compare these tradeoffs before selecting materials.
