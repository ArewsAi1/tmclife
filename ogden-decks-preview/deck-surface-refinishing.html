Deck Surface Refinishing in Ogden

Surface refinishing can restore a weathered wood deck when the framing and most boards remain serviceable. The process begins with cleaning, condition review and preparation—not simply applying another coat.

Refinishing process

Work may include washing, sanding or stripping, tightening fasteners, replacing isolated boards and applying a compatible stain or sealer.

When replacement is better

Soft boards, widespread rot or structural movement may require repair or replacement before finish work is worthwhile.
