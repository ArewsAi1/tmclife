Deck Rail Gate Installation in Ogden

Rail gates help manage access at deck stairs and openings while maintaining the appearance of the surrounding railing.

Compatible gate systems

Gate width, swing direction, latch height, post support and railing compatibility must be planned together. Ogden Decks installs gates for suitable metal, composite, vinyl and custom railing systems.

Safety and access

Gates can support household access goals, but they do not replace supervision or required pool-barrier systems unless specifically designed and approved for that use.
