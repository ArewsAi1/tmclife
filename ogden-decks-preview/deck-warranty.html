Deck Warranty Information

Deck projects may include several separate warranty sources, such as decking, railing, treated lumber, fasteners or foundation products. Coverage, registration and claim procedures vary by manufacturer and product line.

Keep project records

Homeowners should retain the signed contract, invoices, product names, color information, installation records and manufacturer literature supplied for the project.

Confirm current terms

Warranty periods and exclusions can change. Current written manufacturer terms and the signed project agreement control over general website descriptions.
