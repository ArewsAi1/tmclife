What to Look for in Project Photos

Photos are most useful when viewed alongside the project conditions. Deck height, access, framing, stairs, railing, drainage, exposure and the connection to the house all affect the final design and price.

When gathering inspiration, note the board direction, border details, fascia treatment, stair width, railing color and how the deck transitions into the yard. Those details help us understand which parts of a project you want to incorporate into your own design.
