Cocktail Railing for Decks

A cocktail or drink rail uses a wider top surface that can hold small items while remaining part of the deck guard system. Ogden Decks coordinates the rail profile with posts, infill and deck materials.

Design and function

The top rail must be securely supported and selected to work with the railing system, required guard height and exposure to weather.
