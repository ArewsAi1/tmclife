Pergola Over Deck Design

A pergola can add shade and structure to a deck, but the supports and attachment details should be planned with the deck rather than added as an afterthought.

Coordinate the structure

Design considerations include post locations, footing support, railing conflicts, door and window clearances, roofline height and wind exposure.

Plan for useful shade

Orientation, slat direction and optional shade systems should reflect how the deck is used throughout the day.
