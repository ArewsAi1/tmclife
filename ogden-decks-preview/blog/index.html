Use the Blog to Prepare for an Estimate

Before requesting an estimate, gather approximate dimensions, photos of the property, preferred material examples and a list of the features that matter most. For repairs, include visible damage and photos from below the deck whenever safely possible.

The articles provide general planning information. The final design, repair scope and pricing depend on the actual property, structure, access and code requirements.
