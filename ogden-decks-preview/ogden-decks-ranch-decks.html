Ranch-Style Decks in Ogden

Ranch-style homes often work well with broad, lower-profile decks that extend usable space across the back of the house and create direct connections to the yard.

Layout considerations

Door locations, grade changes, long stair runs, shade, railing and transitions to patios or landscaping should be coordinated before framing begins.
