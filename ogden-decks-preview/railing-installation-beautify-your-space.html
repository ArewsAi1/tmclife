Decorative Deck Railing Design

Railing can frame views, define the edge of a deck and coordinate the outdoor space with the home exterior.

Use proportion and sightlines

Post size, top-rail profile, infill spacing and color affect whether the railing feels open, traditional, modern or visually heavy.

Keep appearance tied to performance

Decorative decisions still need to work with structural attachment, stair geometry, gates and long-term maintenance.
