Deck Cleaning Services in Ogden

Cleaning removes surface dirt, organic buildup and weathering so the deck can be inspected and prepared for ongoing maintenance.

Cleaning by material

Wood and composite decking require different products, pressure levels and techniques. The process should protect the board surface, railing and nearby finishes.

Preparation for repair or refinishing

Cleaning can reveal loose boards, fastener problems, coating failure and areas that need repair before stain or sealer is applied.
