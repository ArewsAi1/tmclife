Deck Planning for Eden’s Climate

Mountain properties require more than a standard layout. Snow accumulation, freeze-thaw cycles, sloped sites, access, roof runoff and exposure all influence framing, footings, stairs, railing and material selection.

Projects may include elevated decks, view-oriented layouts, covered areas, hot-tub platforms, replacement decking, structural repairs and low-maintenance composite systems.
