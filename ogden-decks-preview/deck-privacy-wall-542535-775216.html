Deck Privacy Walls in Ogden

Privacy walls can screen nearby properties, reduce visual exposure and create a more defined outdoor room without fully enclosing the deck.

Design options

Options include horizontal or vertical slats, framed panels, partial-height screens and combinations with pergolas or planter boxes.

Wind and attachment planning

Privacy screens create wind load. Post size, framing connections, height and placement are evaluated before installation.
