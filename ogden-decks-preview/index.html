Ogden Deck Builder

Ogden Decks is a premier deck building contractor in and around Ogden, Utah. Our contractors provide free bids, free estimates, and dependable service for homeowners who want to build, repair, expand, or improve an outdoor deck.

We work with wood, Trex, Deckorators and other composite decking products. Each project is planned around the home, the property, the desired use of the deck, railing needs, stairs, shade and long-term maintenance.

Our team can also help with deck repair, power washing, staining, sealing, railing, pergolas, lighting and other outdoor living upgrades.
