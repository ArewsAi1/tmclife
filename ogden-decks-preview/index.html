Welcome

A deck installation gives your home more usable outdoor space for relaxing, entertaining and enjoying Northern Utah. Ogden Decks helps homeowners create dependable outdoor spaces that fit the property, the home and the way the space will be used.

From a new composite deck to repairs, railing, stairs, staining and shade, our team provides practical guidance and professional construction from start to finish.

About Us

Ogden Decks has served homeowners throughout Weber and Davis counties for years. We help with design, material selection, pricing, framing, stairs, railing, shade and the other details that determine how a deck looks and performs.

Our goal is straightforward: build or repair the deck correctly, communicate clearly and leave the customer with an outdoor space they can use with confidence.
