Welcome

A deck installation on your property provides you with more capability and utility outside your home. Whether you envision social gatherings, relaxing after a long day, or enjoying Northern Utah weather on the weekend, a well-built deck gives you a dependable place to do it.

Choosing experienced local deck builders provides both beauty and durability in the finished installation.

About Us

Ogden Decks has provided deck building and repair services throughout the area for many years. Our team helps homeowners understand design, materials, pricing, railing, stairs, shade and the other decisions that shape a successful project.

From the first phone call through the final construction details, we focus on practical planning, dependable craftsmanship and a finished outdoor space that fits the home.
