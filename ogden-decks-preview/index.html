Decking Materials Built for Utah

Trex Composite Decking

Trex is a low-maintenance composite made with recycled wood and plastic. It resists rot, splintering and insect damage while offering a wide range of warm wood tones and modern gray colors. Hidden fasteners and clean edge details create a smooth finished surface without visible screws.

Trex is a strong choice for homeowners who want long-term performance without the recurring sanding, staining and sealing required by many wood decks.

Learn about Trex decking

Deckorators Decking

Deckorators offers mineral-based composite products engineered for strength, dimensional stability and resistance to moisture. Its color and texture options can support traditional, contemporary and mountain-home designs.

We help compare product lines, colors, warranties, framing requirements and railing combinations so the finished deck matches both the property and the homeowner’s maintenance goals.
