Choosing Materials

Composite decking can reduce staining and sealing, while wood offers a traditional appearance and can be refinished. Color, heat exposure, board direction, fascia and railing should be reviewed together before installation.

For existing decks, structural conditions should be checked below the surface. Posts, beams, joists, ledger connections and stairs determine whether repair, resurfacing or replacement is appropriate.
