Deck Planning for Centerville Properties

Centerville homes can involve hillside grades, elevated doorways and views that make structural layout, foundations, stairs and railing especially important. Drainage, access and the relationship between the deck and yard should be resolved before finish materials are selected.

Projects may include new decks, replacement structures, repairs, composite resurfacing, steel or cable railing, pergolas and under-deck systems. Existing decks should be evaluated for movement, moisture damage and connection problems before cosmetic upgrades begin.
