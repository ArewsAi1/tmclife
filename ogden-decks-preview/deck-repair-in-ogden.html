Deck Repair in Ogden, Utah

Ogden Decks repairs unsafe, worn and weather-damaged decks throughout Northern Utah. The first step is identifying whether the problem is cosmetic, localized or structural.

Common repairs

Board replacement, loose railing, stair repair, framing reinforcement, rot removal, fascia replacement, surface refinishing and corrections to drainage or movement problems.

Repair before replacement

When the framing remains serviceable, targeted repairs can preserve the deck and reduce unnecessary replacement costs. When structural conditions are poor, we explain the safer rebuilding options.
