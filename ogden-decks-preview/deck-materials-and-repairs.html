Deck Materials and Repairs

Repair decisions depend on both the condition of the structure and the compatibility of replacement materials.

Match the repair to the material

Wood, capped composite, PVC, metal railing and trim products each require different fasteners, spacing, cleaning and replacement methods.

Look beyond the visible surface

Damaged boards may point to drainage, framing, movement or attachment problems that should be corrected before finish materials are replaced.
