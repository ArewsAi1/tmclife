Materials and Long-Term Use

Composite decking offers lower routine maintenance, while wood provides a traditional appearance and can be refinished. Railing, fascia and border details should be selected alongside the decking so the finished project feels coordinated.

For repair projects, framing and connections should be evaluated before cosmetic upgrades begin. A sound structure may support resurfacing or expansion; widespread deterioration may make replacement more practical.
