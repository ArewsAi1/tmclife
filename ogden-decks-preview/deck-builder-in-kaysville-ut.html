Material and Maintenance Considerations

Composite decking reduces the need for staining and sealing, while wood provides a traditional appearance and can be refinished. Sun exposure, snow, drainage and how often the deck will be used should be considered before selecting a board.

For an existing structure, cosmetic damage should not be evaluated in isolation. Framing, posts, beams, ledger connections and stairs should be checked before new decking or railing is installed.
