Appointments and Maintenance

Arrive with clean lashes and avoid eye makeup before the appointment. Styling and fullness depend on natural lash condition. Regular fills help maintain the set as natural lashes shed.

Follow the artist’s cleansing and aftercare instructions. Tell the provider about allergies, sensitivity or recent eye-area procedures.
