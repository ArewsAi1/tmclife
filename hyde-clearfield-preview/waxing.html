Before and After Your Appointment

Hair should generally be long enough for the wax to grip. Tell the provider about prescription retinoids, recent peels, sunburn, active irritation or other factors that may increase sensitivity.

After waxing, avoid unnecessary heat, friction and harsh exfoliation for the period recommended by the provider. Consistent appointment timing can help maintain results.
