What to Expect

Laser hair removal works across a series of appointments because hair grows in cycles. The number and spacing of sessions vary by area, hair density, skin response and individual goals. A consultation helps establish whether treatment is appropriate and how to prepare.

Clients should follow the provider’s instructions regarding sun exposure, shaving, topical products and post-treatment skin care. Results vary, and maintenance sessions may be recommended.
